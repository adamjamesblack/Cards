Primary palette

Neutrals — only these three

block

Full value onlyNo tints, shades or transparencies of brand colors. If a color feels too strong, use less of it — not a lighter version of it.

filter_1

One color leadsLet a single color carry emphasis per layout. Don't crowd bold colors together.

contrast

No mid-graysSecondary text is black, not gray. Hierarchy comes from size and spacing, never from washed-out tones.

Approved gradients

For large surfaces and hero moments, used sparingly. Never modify the angle or stops, never place two gradients adjacent, never behind small text.

Do and don't

Do

Use the purple logo on white and light gray; the white logo on purple, dark and photographic backgrounds.

Do

Keep clear space around the logo — at least the height of the “H” on every side.

Do

Scale proportionally from the provided SVG or PNG files.

Don't

Recolor the logo — it exists in purple and white only.

Don't

Stretch, condense, rotate, outline or add effects like shadows and glows.

Don't

Place it on low-contrast backgrounds, busy imagery, or a tint of a brand color.

Typography

Roman weight. Tight tracking. Black type.

The brand typeface is Neue Haas Grotesk Text Pro (this page substitutes Inter, the closest free neo-grotesque). Roman 400 is the default almost everywhere — hierarchy comes from size and spacing, not weight.

The human moment

Health and money, handled — so you can focus on what actually matters.

Libre Baskerville Italic adds warmth on a line about care, wellbeing or human impact. One phrase, used sparingly — never for whole paragraphs.

Voice and tone

A modern, personal voice built for real people.

The ease of a modern consumer brand, with the authority of a market leader. Plain language about health and money — optimistic, capable, reassuring.

person

Talk to “you”Empower people by talking to them, not over them. “Take control of your health, your way.”

bolt

Lead with the pointShort and scannable. One idea per line. Break dense info into digestible sections.

verified

Confident, never coldClear, fresh, human. Authoritative without jargon. Real life, slightly elevated.

sentiment_neutral

No emojiThe brand does not use emoji in its voice or UI — anywhere.

Graphics

The Wayfinder.

The one true brand graphic: a rounded chevron with a 45° point that always points right — forward energy, progress, momentum. Fill it with a brand color or approved gradient. Never outline it, never point it anywhere else.

Fill
arrow_forward

Always points rightThe Wayfinder signals forward motion. It is never flipped, rotated or pointed left.

format_paint

Filled, never outlinedUse a brand color or approved gradient fill. No strokes, no hollow versions.

target

Purposeful, not decorativeUse it as a banner, a label, or a frame around imagery — never as background noise.

Photography

Real life, slightly elevated.

Authentic, candid, emotionally grounded imagery in natural or soft light. Lifestyle subjects stay immersed in the moment — they don't look at the camera. Wellness-focused, never clinical.

wb_sunny

Natural, soft lightReal environments people actually live in. No metaphor props, no fabricated visuals.

visibility_off

Immersed, not posedLifestyle subjects don't look at the camera; portraits may. Medical imagery is context, used sparingly.

rounded_corner

12pt corners or full bleedImages get the brand's 12pt rounded corners — or run full-bleed with no frame at all.
