Save

Your emergency shield

A surprise ER bill should never touch your paycheck. Ideal savers automate contributions, capture every employer dollar, and build a tax-free shield before they need it — because the moment you need it is too late to build it.

Spend

Pay less for care

The same scan can cost triple across town, and every dollar you don't overspend stays yours, growing tax-free. Ideal spenders price care like a major purchase, verify what qualifies, and keep every receipt — making healthcare genuinely more affordable.

Invest

Long-term security

Health care is the biggest bill most retirees face. Invested HSA dollars compound for decades with zero tax drag — the difference between an account that pays for prescriptions and one that funds your future security. Cash on the sidelines is the quiet way to miss it.
